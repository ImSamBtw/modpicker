# Homepage showcase

`garage-showcase.webp` is an original AI-generated concept illustration, produced with the built-in image-generation tool and compressed to WebP for the homepage. It is not a community submission and is labeled accordingly in the UI.

Generation prompt: Premium automotive editorial photograph for an automotive build-planning website. A graphite silver American sports coupe with bronze wheels and a forest-green classic pickup, parked at a mountain overlook at blue hour. Entire vehicles visible with cropping margin; realistic bodywork and wheels, warm headlights, restrained slate and silver palette. No people, text, watermark, or UI.

Future community photography should replace the figure's image and caption only after explicit owner opt-in, rights/attribution and moderation are implemented. Keep owner identity and vehicle captions associated with each approved image. A future rotation needs previous/next and pause controls plus reduced-motion support. Do not pull private Garage photos into the homepage. No submissions or autoplay are implemented in this release.
